# Community conduct

Project participation should be professional, respectful, and focused on
scientific and software quality.

Acceptable participation includes constructive review, clear attribution,
reproducible evidence, and good-faith disagreement. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation, plagiarism, and disclosure of
private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove material that violates these expectations and
may restrict repeated disruptive participation. Concerns should be reported
privately through the contact route in SECURITY.md.
