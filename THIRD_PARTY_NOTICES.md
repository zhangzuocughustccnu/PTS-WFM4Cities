# Third-party dependencies

WFM4Cities depends on independently licensed open-source packages. They are
installed from their own distributions and are not copied into this repository.

Core runtime:

- NumPy;
- SciPy;
- Matplotlib;
- scikit-learn.

Optional spatial diagnostics:

- GeoPandas;
- Shapely;
- libpysal;
- esda.

Development and release tools include pytest, pytest-cov, Ruff, build, Twine,
and GitHub Actions maintained by their respective projects.

Users and redistributors are responsible for reviewing the license terms of the
specific dependency versions they install. The MIT license in this repository
applies to WFM4Cities itself, not to third-party packages.
